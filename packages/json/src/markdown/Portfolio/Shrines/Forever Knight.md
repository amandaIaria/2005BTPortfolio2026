## Body

Forever Knight is supernatural police procedural that was one of the key reasons why I became a vampire media fan. It started as a made for TV movie in the late 80s staring Rick Springfield and was going to be a pilot for a tv series. Well, that didn't happen. When it was finally picked up, the city change, 99% of the caste changed, and well the atmosphere changed.

I caught this show on Scifi (back when it was good) and watched it on monday's not knowing that it was on every weekday. It's 1997 middle school me is still trying to figure out who I am and my favorite show is on reruns on scifi. I kind of wonder what my parents thought at the time, but who cares. It's dark, moody, and set up some wish full fillment for me.

Nick is a french 800 year old vampire looking for redemption. He does this by becomeing a cop and "hunting" down evil people. Too bad no one told him what cops where. You'd think someone who is on the edge of humanity would have figured it out.

This show inspired other creative works like Vampire The Masquraide, Angel, Midnight, and a few others that I don't know about. Now that's not to say it's the perfect show; it has it's problems. For instance some of the powers and rules may or may not be consistant.

It's 1992 where's your 800 year old vampire?

### Cast

#### The Vampires

We've got as said before Nicholas, a french vampire from the times of the crusades. He "used" to be evil and being something that you might have found in a Dark Ages Vampire the Masquerade game, but after certain altercations he needed to be saved. He seeks a way to become human again.

Next was Jeanette, a 1000 year old french vampire who was saved and empowered by her maker. As an adult she's become my favorite character. She's sudective, pragmatic, and tired of Nick's shit. She becomes the emotional support for Nick, even in 1000 years she's still doing emotional labor for men.

Their maker was Lucian LaCroix, a vampire from antiquity who was a witness to the eruption of Mount Vesuvius at Pompeii. If it wasn't for his maker he would have died and been another corpse buried by ash. His relationship, to not spoil to much, is the opposite of Lestat and Gabrielle. It was not something he wanted.

#### The Humans

Don Schanke is Nick's homicide partner. He's what I would think a vice cop from the 80s would be like. He's annoying, in your face, and can be rather off putting at times. His actor is the only one to originally be from the tv movie.

A change from the TV movie (which I think was the best idea), was to make Nick's bestie and person who is trying to make him human, was to make them a woman. Natalie is a forensic scientist who met Nick one night at the morgue. He was just a body that was served a pipe bomb, but instead of a corpse she met a vampire. She becomes a love interest for Nick, which doesn't end well.

In the last season Don is killed in a bombing of a air plane. Nick's new partner is Tracy Vetter, a cop from a family of cops who just wants to strike it herself. There's a tale of nepotism that often feels like they could have gone more into, but never really do.

### Seasons

#### season one

Where we meet our caste of characters. This season is more supernatural then police procedural and we slowly learn about the vampire world. Some of the best atmospher can be found in this season.

#### season two

This season has some of my favorite episodes in it. There's a fever dream where Nick's guilt is so powerful that he hallucinates a life as a human. Another where Nick has to save Nat from Lacroix or else she will be turned, which it's a bit of the "save the woman" troupe of the 90s.

#### season three

Season three is well a season. It has some "choices" from vampire dogs to a fever dream episoide where HIV/AIDS save's vampire kind from their own epidemics. It's a choice is what I can say.
